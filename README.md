# 🌍 WanderPlan Studio - Pro Travel Itinerary & Trip Planner

An advanced, responsive Travel Itinerary Planner, Universal Trip Generator, and AI Trip Concierge web application.

## ✨ All Included Features

1. **Universal AI Itinerary Generator (Any Destination & Any Day Count)**:
   - **Type ANY Destination in the World**: Type any city, region, or country (e.g. *Kashmir, Dubai, Switzerland, Ladakh, Goa, Ooty, London, Bali, Darjeeling, Amalfi Coast, Tokyo, Pondicherry, Baku, etc.*) or click quick presets.
   - **Custom Day Count (1 to 14 Days)**: Select the exact duration you want (e.g. 3-day weekend getaway, 6-day scenic holiday, or 14-day grand expedition).
   - **Currency & Budget Tier**: Choose INR (₹), USD ($), EUR (€), or GBP (£), along with your budget tier (*Budget Explorer, Balanced Comfort, Luxury & Heritage*).
   - **Automatic Intelligent Synthesis**: Generates day-by-day morning, afternoon, and evening activities with realistic timing, venues, costs, climate-specific packing lists, weather forecasts, and hotel/flight bookings!

2. **100% Full Customization Freedom**:
   - **Every Activity is Editable**: Click **"Edit"** on any activity to tweak the title, time, location, category, cost, and personal notes.
   - **Delete or Reorder**: Easily remove activities or reorder them across days.
   - **Add New Activities**: Click **"+ Add Activity"** on any day to add flights, reservations, meals, or excursions.
   - **Add or Remove Days**: Scale your itinerary on the fly by clicking **"+ Add Day"** or removing days.
   - **Editable Day Themes**: Rename days or change themes (e.g., "Day 3: Sunset Boat Cruise & Seafood Feast").
   - **Custom Bookings & Rescheduling**: Add, edit, or delete hotel reservations, train tickets, and flights in the Bookings Vault.

3. **WanderBot AI Travel Concierge (Interactive Chat Bot)**:
   - **Floating & Header Access**: Always accessible via bottom-right floating badge or top navigation bar.
   - **Context-Aware Assistance**: Automatically detects the active trip, current day, destination, and budget buffer.
   - **Interactive 1-Click Itinerary Insertion**: When recommending restaurants or attractions, users can click **`[+ Add to Day X Itinerary]`** directly inside the chat bubble to insert the item into their active day with live pricing!
   - **Instant Packing Insertion**: Recommend gear for current weather with **`[+ Add to Packing List]`** buttons.
   - **Live Budget Health Audits**: Ask *"How is my budget?"* to receive a real-time audit comparing planned spend vs remaining budget buffer.
   - **Culture & Transit Wisdom**: Authentic answers on temple dress codes, UPI scan payments (GPay/PhonePe), IRCTC train tips, and local emergency helplines.

4. **Visual Route & Waypoint Map**:
   - Dynamic SVG route path connecting daily attractions in sequence with stop numbers.
   - Waypoint inspection card with transit estimates, times, and venue landmarks.

5. **Bookings & Reservations Vault**:
   - Centralized management for Flights, Hotels, Trains, Car Rentals, and Tour Passes.
   - Track confirmation codes (PNR), dates, terminal/gate info, and total pre-trip booking expenses.

6. **Interactive Day-by-Day Scheduler**:
   - Time slots (*Morning, Afternoon, Evening*).
   - Category tags: **Sightseeing, Dining, Transport, Lodging, Activity / Tour, Shopping**.
   - Check off completed activities, view locations, estimated costs, and booking notes.

7. **Live Budget & Expense Analyzer**:
   - Real-time expense aggregation combining daily itinerary activities and reservations.
   - Visual budget progress bar and remaining balance indicator (with over-budget alert).
   - Per-person cost and average daily spend breakdown.
   - Itemized expense table and category distribution bars.

8. **Trip Journal & Memories**:
   - Log memorable moments, stories, culinary discoveries, and mood tags (*✨ Breathtaking, 🍜 Delicious, 😌 Peaceful, 🧗 Adventurous, 🎉 Exciting*).

9. **Destination Weather Widget**:
   - Destination temperature, sky conditions, and contextual clothing advisories.

10. **Luggage & Packing Checklist**:
    - Categorized gear tracking: **Documents, Clothing, Electronics, Toiletries, Misc**.
    - Progress bar showing packed items and quick item creation.

11. **Travel Toolkit**:
    - Multi-currency converter (USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, INR, AUD).
    - Emergency contact numbers (Police, Ambulance, Tourist Helpline).

12. **Import, Export & Sharing**:
    - **Export JSON**: Download your itinerary for backup or sharing.
    - **Import JSON**: Drag-and-drop or paste an itinerary JSON to restore it immediately.
    - **Copy Markdown**: One-click formatted Markdown summary ready to paste into Notion, Obsidian, WhatsApp, or email.
    - **Print-Ready**: Clean print layout for saving as PDF or printing travel binders.

13. **Automatic Persistence**:
    - Saves all trip edits, check-marks, and custom additions directly into browser `localStorage`.
